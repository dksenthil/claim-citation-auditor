# Benchmark manuscript (labelled)

A regression fixture for the claim-citation-auditor. Each numbered claim is
engineered to exercise one verdict class. The references are REAL papers (so
`retrieve_evidence.py` resolves them) except the sixth entry, which is a
deliberate fabrication. Gold verdicts are in `benchmark_gold.json`.

## Body

Nucleoside-modified mRNA suppresses innate immune activation and increases protein
translation, a foundational result for mRNA therapeutics [1]. The codon adaptation
index (CAI) is a strong and general predictor of protein expression across
biological systems [2]. mRNA secondary structure has essentially no effect on
protein output [3]. The LinearDesign algorithm improved mRNA stability and
immunogenicity, including in animal models [4]. Deep-learning methods have now
solved RNA three-dimensional structure prediction to the same degree as proteins
[5]. Global regulatory agencies have issued a single unified, legally binding law
governing all AI use in drug manufacturing [6]. Synonymous codon substitutions were
shown in 2018 to perturb co-translational protein folding in vivo [7].
